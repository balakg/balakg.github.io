The following are the major research themes in my group.

---

### <i class="fa-solid fa-wave-square" style="color: #4A90D9;"></i> Neural Signal Representations

We develop compact neural representations of signals and images, covering architecture design, multiscale modeling, and transferable priors across data modalities.

**Selected papers:** WIRE ([CVPR 2023](https://vishwa91.github.io/wire)), MINER ([ECCV 2022](https://vishwa91.github.io/miner)), Transferable INRs ([NeurIPS 2024](https://kushalvyas.github.io/strainer.html)), Bias for Action ([CVPR 2025](https://alpoler.github.io/actioner)), Noise Pretraining ([CVPR 2026](https://kushalvyas.github.io/noisepretraining.html)), Fit Pixels, Get Labels ([MICCAI 2025](https://kushalvyas.github.io/metaseg.html))

---

### <i class="fa-solid fa-stethoscope" style="color: #D95B5B;"></i> Medical Imaging

We develop deep learning methods for clinical image analysis, including deformable image registration, probabilistic segmentation, and sparse-view CT reconstruction.

**Selected papers:** VoxelMorph ([TMI 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.05231), [CVPR 2018](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Balakrishnan_An_Unsupervised_Learning_CVPR_2018_paper.html), [MedIA 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.03545)), Data Augmentation ([CVPR 2019](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Zhao_Data_Augmentation_Using_Learned_Transformations_for_One-Shot_Medical_Image_Segmentation_CVPR_2019_paper.html)), CT Reconstruction ([MICCAI 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.02100)), COMPASS ([ICLR 2026](https://openreview.net/forum?id=uBy4TCgGiT)), Fit Pixels, Get Labels ([MICCAI 2025](https://kushalvyas.github.io/metaseg.html))

---

### <i class="fa-solid fa-cube" style="color: #9B6EC8;"></i> 3D Reconstruction and Scene Synthesis

We reconstruct and synthesize 3D scenes and objects from images and video — including buildings, face avatars, relightable objects, and crowd-free environments.

**Selected papers:** DRAGON ([ICCP 2024](https://yujinh22.github.io/publication/dragon)), TranSplat ([2025](https://tonyyu0822.github.io/transplat/)), SplatShot ([2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01493)), Humanless Walkthroughs ([CVPR 2026](https://crowd-eraser.github.io)), GIQ ([ICLR 2026](https://toomanymatts.github.io/giq-benchmark/))

---

### <i class="fa-solid fa-scale-balanced" style="color: #4EAA7A;"></i> Bias and Reliability in Vision

We study bias and reliability in visual recognition and medical AI systems, developing causal benchmarks, tools to surface dataset biases, and conformal prediction methods for uncertainty quantification.

**Selected papers:** Causal Benchmarking ([ECCV 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.06570)), Leveling Down ([CVPR 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.04913)), Bias Benchmarking ([ICCV 2023](https://hliang2.github.io/BenchmarkingReco/)), GELDA ([CVPR 2024](https://krishk97.github.io/publication/gelda)), COMPASS ([ICLR 2026](https://openreview.net/forum?id=uBy4TCgGiT)), Not All Views Equal ([ICCV 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Michalkiewicz_Not_all_Views_are_Created_Equal_Analyzing_Viewpoint_Instabilities_in_ICCV_2025_paper.html)), GIQ ([ICLR 2026](https://toomanymatts.github.io/giq-benchmark/))

---

### <i class="fa-solid fa-satellite" style="color: #E8943A;"></i> Environmental Sensing and Geospatial AI

We develop machine learning methods for environmental monitoring, including precipitation downscaling, land surface temperature estimation, disaster damage assessment, and multimodal geospatial representation.

**Selected papers:** ISLAND ([Remote Sensing Applications 2024](https://github.com/way-yuhao/island)), Precipitation Downscaling ([TGRS 2025](https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2025.3611872)), Debris Segmentation ([CAICE 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12542)), GeoViSTA ([2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14406))
