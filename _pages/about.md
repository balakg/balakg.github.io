My group works on two main research threads: **efficient representation of visual and scientific signals**, and **bias and uncertainty quantification for vision algorithms**. We often apply our methods to the domains of medical imaging, environmental science, and 3D scene reconstruction.

I completed my PhD at [MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/) advised by [John Guttag](http://people.csail.mit.edu/guttag/) and [Frédo Durand](http://people.csail.mit.edu/fredo/), followed by postdoctoral research with [Bill Freeman](https://billf.mit.edu/). I received dual B.S. degrees in Computer Science and Computer Engineering from the [University of Michigan, Ann Arbor](https://umich.edu/). I am a member of the [Rice Digital Health Initiative](https://digitalhealth.rice.edu/) and the [Houston Methodist–Rice Digital Health Institute](https://www.houstonmethodist.org/research/).
