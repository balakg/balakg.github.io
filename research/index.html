research

Current research themes of my group:

Neural Signal Representations

Building practical implicit neural representations: scalable to large and complex signals, transferable across instances, and applicable to medical, geospatial, and video data.

Selected papers: WIRE (CVPR 2023), MINER (ECCV 2022), Transferable INRs (NeurIPS 2024), Video INRs (CVPR 2025), Noise Pretrained INRs (CVPR 2026), Fit Pixels, Get Labels (MICCAI 2025), ISLAND (Remote Sensing Applications 2024), GeoViSTA (2026)

Bias and Reliability of AI Systems

Diagnosing and correcting bias in AI systems through causal analysis, robustness probing, and conformal uncertainty quantification.

Selected papers: Causal Benchmarking of Face Analysis (ECCV 2020, ICCV 2023), GELDA (CVPR 2024), Precipitation Downscaling (TGRS 2025), COMPASS (ICLR 2026), Not All Views Equal (ICCV 2025), GIQ (ICLR 2026)

Scene Reconstruction

Reconstructing and synthesizing 3D scenes, objects, and volumes from images and video, including human avatars, urban environments, and sparse-view medical imaging.

Selected papers: CT Reconstruction (MICCAI 2023), DRAGON (ICCP 2024), TranSplat (2025), SplatShot (2026), Humanless Walkthroughs (CVPR 2026), GIQ (ICLR 2026)
